WinMine 98 is an independent, native recreation created for this project.
No Microsoft binaries or sound files are included.

The fixed Windows 98 window samples were derived from the reference image
provided by the user for this project.

Number, mine, question-mark, and alternate face glyphs are adapted from the
MIT-licensed AlexAegis/minesweeper project. Its complete license is included
as AlexAegis-Minesweeper-LICENSE.txt.

The pixelated MS Sans Serif and MS Sans Serif Bold typefaces are FontStruct
recreations by "lou", distributed under Creative Commons Attribution-
ShareAlike 3.0. Their license notices are included alongside this file.
