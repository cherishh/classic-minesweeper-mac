Classic Minesweeper

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

No data collection

Classic Minesweeper does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app contains no analytics, advertising, accounts, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your Mac

The app stores game preferences and best times locally in its macOS app container. This information never leaves your device. You can clear best times inside the app; uninstalling the app and removing its container deletes the remaining local data.

Network access

The game works entirely offline and does not request network access. Opening the Privacy Policy or Support command launches these pages in your default web browser.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted on this page. Questions can be submitted through GitHub Issues.
